Anthropic opens a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard

A shared driver spec for agents to operate programmable lab and factory devices, starting with a closed partner group.

PUBLISHED 27 AUG 2026 STATUS VERIFIED

Summary

Anthropic published a primary announcement that MHS is in research preview with scientific labs and advanced manufacturers. The standard is model-agnostic, talks to existing programmable interfaces, and is intended to be open-sourced after safety work with partners.

Read

This is the originating signal for the current edition. It is a company announcement, not an independent audit of the standard. The claims to carry forward are: research-preview status, programmable-interface requirement, model-agnostic access via MCP/CLI/API, origin at HHMI Janelia, and an explicit plan to open-source later — not immediately.
