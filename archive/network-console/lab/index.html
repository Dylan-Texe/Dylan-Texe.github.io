ABOUT THE BUILDER

Learn by building.

I learn technology by taking systems apart, building them back up, testing them and documenting what happens.

My focus is practical IT and systems work: infrastructure, networking, Linux and Windows administration, virtualization, automation and troubleshooting. The goal is not to collect theory — it is to build systems, understand why they work, and keep a reliable record of how they were built.

The lab is now moving beyond the Windows infrastructure build into networking and broader systems projects, with each project documented as both a working experiment and a reusable technical reference.
